The active deck uses the two Bicycle images supplied by the user for this task:

- `../cards/bicycle-sheet.png`: exact face and printed-joker artwork from the supplied uncut sheet.
- `../cards/bicycle-back-reference.png`: supplied red Bicycle back photograph.

Bicycle artwork and branding belong to The United States Playing Card Company.
These supplied references are not covered by the CC0 dedication below. The source
images are preserved, and SVG windows select each face. The unobscured right half
of the back is mapped into a rectangle and reflected to complete the symmetrical
pattern, since the ace partly covers the left half in the supplied photograph.

The older, currently unused `cards.svg` and `card-artwork.json` deck is adapted
from Adrian Kennard's public-domain playing cards, distributed under CC0 1.0.
