The active deck uses image-generated restorations of the supplied Bicycle artwork:

- `../cards/bicycle-sheet-enhanced.png`: restored face sheet, with uniform rich red/black suits, brighter court colors and clearer indices.
- `../cards/bicycle-back-enhanced.png`: complete upright red Rider Back, restored from the partly obscured supplied photograph.
- `../cards/enhancement-prompts.md`: final prompts used with the built-in image-generation tool on 13 October 2026.

The original `bicycle-sheet.png` and `bicycle-back-reference.png` are preserved
as references. Bicycle artwork and branding belong to The United States Playing
Card Company. These references and their enhanced derivatives are not covered by
the CC0 dedication below. The face bitmap maps into the original logical sprite
canvas; the back is a complete image and no longer needs mirrored photograph
fragments. All 36 numbered faces were checked for their correct pip counts.

The older, currently unused `cards.svg` and `card-artwork.json` deck is adapted
from Adrian Kennard's public-domain playing cards, distributed under CC0 1.0.
